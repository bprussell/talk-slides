What we actually hire for now

Judgment

Breadth

Smaller teams. Wear the analyst and tester hats too.

Pragmatism

Not a holdout. Not a zealot.

Curiosity

Especially early in a career.

Why isn't that on the posting?

1

You can't advertise for judgment.

Most people believe they have it. So we test for it in the interview.

2

Postings are kept up by people with other jobs.

Expectations move faster than anyone has time to rewrite them. Ours hasn't fully caught up.
